About the App

Expense Note is a minimalist, privacy-first expense tracking app built with Flutter. It runs entirely on your device — no account, no cloud sync, no data ever leaves your phone.

Record daily expenses and income, assign them to custom categories, and watch your spending patterns come alive through a radial sunburst chart, monthly lollipop chart, and a 30-day mini bar chart right on the home screen.

When you're ready, export your data as a clean PDF report or a CSV spreadsheet for any date range you choose.

Key Features

  • Home Dashboard — Scrollable hero card with live monthly total and a 30-day spending bar chart
  • Sunburst Pie Chart — Tap any category segment to expand it and see the amount in the center
  • Monthly Analytics — Lollipop chart showing 12 months of history at a glance
  • Custom Categories — Create, rename, and reorder your own spending categories
  • Income Tracking — Log income entries alongside expenses, with clear +/− display
  • Multi-Currency — Choose from dozens of global currencies with correct symbols
  • PDF & CSV Export — Generate reports for current month, last month, or any custom date range
  • Smart Notifications — Daily digest, weekly insights, and monthly summaries
  • No Internet Required — Fully offline, data stored locally on device using Hive

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

🔒 Expense Note collects zero personal data. Everything stays on your device. We never see your expenses, income, or any other information.

Data We Collect

Nothing. The app stores all your data locally on your device using an embedded database (Hive). No data is transmitted to any server, third party, or cloud service.

Local Storage

All expense and income records, custom categories, settings (currency, name, notification preferences), and profile image are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all this data.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app schedules local push notifications (daily digest, weekly insights, monthly summary) entirely on-device. No notification data is sent externally.

Permissions

  • Notifications — To deliver local spending reminders (optional)
  • Storage / Files — To save exported PDF and CSV reports to your Downloads folder
  • Camera / Gallery — Only if you choose to set a profile photo (optional)

No permissions are used for tracking, analytics, or advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

Expense Note uses no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking libraries. The only external dependency is Google Fonts, loaded at build time — no font requests are made at runtime.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the new version will be posted here. Continued use of the app means you accept any updated terms.
